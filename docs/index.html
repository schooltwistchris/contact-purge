What it finds

Modern Android stopped tracking "last contacted" data for apps. Contact Purge works around that by filtering on things you can actually see — plus an optional smart sort that reads your call log locally.

🗒️

No phone or email

Contacts you can't actually reach — stray names with no way to contact them.

📡

Service shortcodes

Carrier-added entries like #BAL, *611, balance checkers, data trackers.

👯

Duplicates

Same person, multiple cards. Surface them so you can pick which one to keep.

✨

Smart sort (optional)

Opt in to call-history access and the app surfaces contacts you haven't actually spoken to in years. The data never leaves your phone.

Install in 30 seconds

No app store needed.

  1. On your Android phone, tap the latest release and download the .apk file.
  2. Open the download. Android will warn about "unknown sources" — tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source. This is normal for any app outside the Play Store.
  3. Tap Install. The app appears as Contact Purge in your drawer.
  4. On first launch, grant Contacts permission. Smart sort is optional and prompted separately.

About your data

The whole reason this app exists is that contact cleanup tools shouldn't need a cloud account. So it doesn't have one.

  • Local-only. Your contacts and call log are read on your phone and stay on your phone.
  • No backend. No servers, no API calls, no accounts to create.
  • No analytics. No tracking SDKs, no crash reporting, no telemetry of any kind.
  • No ads, ever. The app is free because it's a hobby project, not a funnel.
  • Open source. The code is public — verify any of the above yourself.
